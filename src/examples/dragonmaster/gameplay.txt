DRAGONMASTER
Cross-Lib version
by Fabrizio Caruso

Original game by David Berdan

GAMEPLAY:
- The goal
You need to defeat 4 king dragons to save your princess.
In order to kill a king dragon no other dragon has to be present.
If there are dragons left, the king dragon is invincible.

- The dragons 
The chest of the dragons is the only vulnerable part.
Dragons instantly kill you if you are directly in front of their mouth or above their heads.
Dragons do not move but can randomly flip from one side to the other.
Moreover new dragons can be spawned randomly. So, you need to be fast.

- The wizard
As the game progresses an invincible wizard will chase you at an increasing speed.

- Scoring
25 points for a dragon
50/100/150/200 for the king dragon
150/100/50/0 as time bonus

- Extra lives
An extra life is awarded every 1000 points.
